Z80 + MSX MEMORY INTEGRATION (12_z80, files z80_msx_sys.v, z80_msx_top.v, sdram_byte_if.v)
==================================================================================
z80_msx_sys.v   Z80 (z80_top) + msx_mem (module 10) + byte bridge. Accesses that fall in the mapper/MegaRAM (hit) go to the physical port mp_*;
                the rest (BIOS ROM, other slots, peripherals) goes out through xm_* (memory) and xio_* (I/O), with x_wait_n. xio_ack=1 on INT acknowledge (xio_rdata = vector).
sdram_byte_if.v  mp_* (1 byte, 24-bit physical address) -> sdram_ctrl (64-bit word + byte-enable). Physical addresses limited to 4 MB (1 MB mapper at 0, 1 MB MegaRAM at 100000h).
z80_msx_top.v   sys + adapter + sdram_ctrl; the Z80 stays in reset until the SDRAM init_done (sdram_ok).
WAIT and DATA are registered (1 clock): requires Z80 ce pulses separated by >= 3 clocks (at 57.27 MHz it holds with turbo 0-2 = 3.58/7.16/14.3 MHz; turbo 3 is limited to 14.3 MHz by z80_ce. At 100 MHz all 4 turbos held, up to 28.6 MHz. With use_ext, ce_ext obeys the same limit).
Test: sim_msx\rodar_z80_msx.bat (turbo 0..3). Program ref\t_msx.z80 (assembled by zasm.py with ZASM_ORG0=1): mapper (remap pages, alias, port read with bits 1),
LDIR between segments, MegaRAM ASCII8 (write mode via IN 8Eh, ROM via OUT 8Eh, banks by window). Expected: "PASS: integration ..." in each turbo.
LIMITS: expanded slots (FFFFh) not implemented; the Z80 reads/writes the SDRAM in ~10-20 clocks per byte (wait states): at high turbo the speed gain is limited by the SDRAM.
