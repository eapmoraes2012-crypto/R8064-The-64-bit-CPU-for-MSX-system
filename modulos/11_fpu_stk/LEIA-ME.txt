MODULE 11 - Residual FPU hardware (fpu_stk): ST0-ST7 stack (80 b), tags, CW, SW, FXAM. The arithmetic is firmware (module 08, ref/fpu_fw.py).
Simulated: 20,000 vectors against an independent Python model (sim/fpu_stk_ref.py): PASS 0 errors (Icarus). Synthesis: 705 LUT1-4 + 19 ALU + 40 RAM16SDP4 (~884 equivalent LUT4), 267 FF, 98.1 MHz with wrapper.
Run: rodar_stk.bat inside the OSS CAD Suite terminal (generates resultado_stk.txt).
Interface: comandos 0 NOP 1 INIT 2 PUSH 3 POP 4 XCH(ST(ra_idx)<->ST(rb_idx)) 5 WRST 6 INCSTP 7 DECSTP 8 SETCW 9 CLREX 10 ORFLG 11 SETCC 12 SETSW 13 SETTW 14 FREE.
Decisions: POP on an empty stack with IM masked increments TOP; unsupported formats = special tag and FXAM without bits; FINIT does not erase data; FXAM uses port A.
Not covered: unmasked exceptions (only ES/B signalling), complete FSAVE/FRSTOR (firmware with WRST/SETSW/SETTW), FPU address registers (FIP/FDP/FOP).
