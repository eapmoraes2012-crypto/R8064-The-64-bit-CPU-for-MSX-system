IMPLICATIONS FOR THE PIPELINE
  1) MLTxB/W/L (EX=1) only keeps a throughput of 1 per clock; the result comes out 5 clocks after issue (X+M+W cover 3): a dependent instruction has to wait ~2 extra clocks
     (or the pipeline needs multiply bypass/extra stages). MLTSQ/MLTUQ: 8 clocks of latency, 4 of occupancy.
  2) The divider occupies X for sz+2 / sz+7 clocks (REV44: single negator; before sz+4); on overflow it finishes in 2-3 clocks (divide exception: define vector/flag).
  3) The 4 DSPs used by the multiplier are the same ones the FPU (64-bit mantissa) and the MMU will need: share or reserve (48 available).
ASSUMPTIONS TO CONFIRM (the spreadsheet does not specify): result of the MLT (e.g.: MLTSB AL,u -> AX; MLTUW HL,rr -> 32 bits in which pair; MLTUQ QA,rr generates 128 bits and which half stays in QA);
  division: truncation toward zero, remainder with the sign of the dividend, error on zero divisor or out-of-range quotient (as on x86); MLT/DIV flags not modeled.
